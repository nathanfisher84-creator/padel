# Competitive audit: CoachMyPadel vs PadelPro

*Compiled 11 Jul 2026. Sources: coachmypadel.com pages (via search index — their
CDN blocks direct crawling), Padel Magazine coverage, and competitor sites
surfaced during research (thepadelschool.com, onlinepadelcoaching.com).*

---

## 1. What CoachMyPadel actually is

**Positioning:** "The leading platform for online technical padel lessons &
tactical match analysis with Premier Padel coaches." Their entire brand is
built on one asset: **elite coach credibility**.

**Model:** a *closed, curated roster* — not a marketplace. Known coaches:

| Coach | Credibility signals they publish |
| --- | --- |
| Ángel González | Premier Padel coach since 2019; **Coach of the Year 2023** (World Padel Awards); coached Ari Sánchez to **world #1** |
| Jorge Bellmont | Head coach Qatar national team (2021), then Saudi Arabia; coached Rico brothers to world top-30 |
| Jesus Canal | Premier Padel circuit coach |
| Thibault Lalouette | French Federation TFP diploma; worked with ~10 Premier Padel coaches |
| Ramiro Choya | Former Spanish #1; coach of Paquito Navarro |

**Products (heavily productized):**
1. **Technical shot analysis** — menu of **20+ named shots** (bandeja, víbora,
   smash, wall return…). Player films the shot from 2–3 angles, ≤1 minute,
   following their filming guidelines. Coach replies with a **3–5 minute video
   breakdown**.
2. **Tactical match analysis** — player uploads 20–30 min of match play, adds
   comments about problem situations. Coach replies with a **10–15 minute
   personalized video**.
3. **3-month program** — 6 shot analyses (2/month) + 1 tactical match analysis
   + 2 video calls (start/end).
4. **6-month program** — 12 shot analyses + tactical analysis + 3 video calls.

**Mechanics & marketing:**
- Turnaround: **7–15 days** (technical), **7–21 days** (tactical)
- Feedback format: **video replies**, with diagrams
- Bilingual EN/FR; Spanish coaches who "speak clear English"
- SEO landing pages per coach, per country (`/padel-coaches/spain/`,
  `/padel-coaches-in-france/`), per segment (`/premier-padel-circuit/`)
- Active YouTube + Instagram; earned press in Padel Magazine
- Pricing is **not publicly indexed** (likely gated on page or by inquiry)

## 3. What they do better than us today (learn)

1. **Credibility architecture.** Every coach page leads with verifiable
   accolades ("Coach of the Year 2023", "coached the world #1"). Our profiles
   have headline + bio + years — no structured credentials, no
   certifications, no career results.
2. **Productized analysis.** "Pick one of 20 shots, film 1 minute from 2–3
   angles" is a far more *legible* product than our generic "upload a video."
   The player knows exactly what to do and exactly what they'll get back.
3. **Filming guidance.** They tell players how to film (angles, duration,
   what to include). Our upload form accepts anything, which produces
   worse inputs for coaches and worse outcomes for players.
4. **Video feedback.** A 3–5 minute personal video is a more premium artifact
   than written text. We only support written feedback — despite already
   having all the video storage infrastructure.
5. **Programs with cadence.** 3/6-month programs with scheduled video calls
   create commitment, higher order value, and a finish line. We sell only
   one-offs and a rolling monthly.
6. **SEO surface area.** Landing pages per coach, country and circuit segment
   compound organic traffic. We have one directory page.

## 4. Where they are weak (attack — improve, don't copy)

1. **Speed: 7–21 days turnaround.** This is their softest spot. Nobody waits
   three weeks happily. A marketplace with coach-committed SLAs (48–72h) and a
   visible "avg. delivery" stat beats this decisively.
2. **Opaque pricing.** No indexed prices; friction before purchase. We show
   exact prices on every card — keep and amplify this.
3. **No player reviews/ratings.** Their trust is all coach-résumé, zero
   customer voice. A working review system (only from verified purchasers)
   is trust they cannot fake.
4. **Closed supply.** Five-ish coaches can't scale, can't cover languages or
   time zones, and creates a bottleneck (see: 21-day turnarounds). Our open
   supply side is a structural advantage — if we add quality control.
5. **Rigid programs.** 6 months upfront is a big ask. Flexible programs
   (cancel anytime, pause) improve on this.
6. **No product surface for coaches.** They don't recruit; we onboard coaches
   with photo/intro video in minutes. That's our growth engine.

## 5. Improvement roadmap for PadelPro

### Tier 1 — trust & legibility (highest impact)
- **Shot taxonomy**: player picks focus shots at upload (bandeja, víbora,
  smash, defence off the glass…); coach sees them; shots become profile tags
  ("specialises in: bandeja, transition") and future SEO pages.
- **Filming guide**: a short checklist inside the upload flow (angles,
  duration, lighting) + a dedicated "how to film" page.
- **Turnaround SLA**: coaches commit to a response time (24h/48h/72h/7d),
  shown on card + profile; measured against actual deliveries; late = credit.
  Market it: "Feedback in days, not weeks."
- **Structured credentials**: certifications, career highlights, languages
  spoken, playing level coached — structured fields, rendered as a
  scannable "Credentials" block.
- **Player reviews**: 1–5 stars + comment, only after a delivered review;
  aggregate on cards ("★ 4.9 · 27 reviews").

### Tier 2 — product depth
- **Video feedback**: let coaches attach a reply video (infra already exists)
  alongside written notes; position as "video-first feedback, in days."
- **Coach-defined packages**: optional 4/8/12-review bundles with a video
  call, monthly cadence, cancel anytime — our flexible answer to their
  rigid programs.
- **Money-back guarantee** on a player's first review.

### Tier 3 — growth
- **SEO pages**: per-coach (exists), per-country, per-shot ("improve your
  bandeja — video analysis by pro coaches").
- **Comparison framing** on the landing page: delivery time, transparent
  pricing, choose-your-coach vs "typical online coaching."
- **Content loop**: anonymized before/after clips (with consent) as social
  proof and social content.

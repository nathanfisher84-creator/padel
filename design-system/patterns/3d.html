3D scenes

Live WebGL objects — three.js via react-three-fiber

The hero's padel ball is a real 3D object (src/components/three/PadelBall3D.tsx): a felt sphere in champagne (ball-400) with crossed rose seams (court-600), lit in the site's own palette. Use it as the template for any new 3D scene.

  • Mount with next/dynamic + ssr:false — three.js stays out of the server bundle and the critical path.
  • Decorative scenes are aria-hidden with pointer-events-none, and hidden on small screens (hidden lg:block) to spare phone batteries.
  • Gate motion behind prefers-reduced-motion (see usePrefersReducedMotion): reduced ⇒ frameloop="demand", no animation.
  • Cap pixel ratio at dpr={[1,2]}; light scenes with palette colors (cream ambient, blush fill) so objects sit in the page.
  • Build geometry procedurally (sphere, torus, lathe) before reaching for model files; keep materials meshStandardMaterial with palette hexes.
